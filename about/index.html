FAQ

Questions business owners ask me.

What services do you offer?

Google Ads management (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Demand Gen, Display and YouTube), conversion tracking with GTM, GA4 and Google Ads, Shopify and booking-system tracking, and account audits with a growth strategy.

Which platforms and campaign types do you work with?

Google Ads Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Demand Gen, Display and YouTube (TrueView), with Merchant Center product feeds. For measurement: Google Tag Manager, GA4, Google Ads conversions and Enhanced Conversions, Microsoft Clarity, and Meta Pixel with the Conversions API for lead tracking.

Can you fix my conversion tracking in GA4 and GTM?

Yes. I set up and repair tracking in Google Tag Manager, GA4 and Google Ads for forms, calls, bookings, purchases, chatbot and WhatsApp clicks, then validate every event in Tag Assistant or GA4 DebugView before any ad decision relies on it.

Will I work with you directly?

Yes. I work as a solo freelancer, so you deal with me directly from the first audit to the reporting.

Do I need to share my Google Ads password?

No. I send an access request from my Google Ads manager account, you approve it, and you keep full ownership of your account and data. You can remove my access at any time.

Which industries have you worked with?

E-commerce stores, lead generation for local and professional services (dental, HVAC, plumbing, AC repair, cleaning, electronics and boat repair, real estate), hospitality (an airport hotel and a wellness resort), conversion tracking projects, and YouTube channel growth.
