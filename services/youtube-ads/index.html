FAQ

Common questions.

Can YouTube ads grow my subscribers?

They can help. Results depend on your videos, audience and budget; I start with low-cost views and then optimise toward subscriber growth where the account allows.

Do I need professionally produced videos?

No. Clear, engaging videos that hook viewers in the first few seconds often work well. I'll tell you honestly which videos look strongest for ads.

How do you stop ads showing on irrelevant videos?

With audience targeting plus placement, content and topic exclusions, reviewed as data comes in.
