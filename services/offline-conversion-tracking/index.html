What I do

What's included.

  • Capture the click ID (GCLID and related IDs) with each form or call lead
  • Pass it into your CRM or lead sheet alongside the contact details
  • Define lead stages to import, such as qualified lead, booked or closed sale
  • Set up imports into Google Ads, by upload, Google Sheets or a CRM connection where available
  • Add conversion values so bidding can favour higher-value leads
  • Check that imported conversions match up with the original clicks

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Map the funnel

    We agree which lead stages matter and where they are recorded today.

  2. Capture IDs

    Forms and call tracking are set up to store the click ID with each lead.

  3. Import

    Lead outcomes are sent back to Google Ads on a regular schedule.

  4. Optimise

    Once enough data builds up, bidding can move toward qualified leads and sales.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which CRMs can this work with?

Any CRM or spreadsheet that can store the click ID with the lead. Some have direct Google Ads connections; others work through scheduled uploads or Google Sheets.

How long until bidding uses the imported data?

It depends on how many offline conversions you import. Smart Bidding needs a steady volume, so I set expectations after seeing your lead numbers.

Can I track phone leads that close later?

Yes, if the call can be tied to the ad click, for example through call tracking or a form submitted before the call. I review your setup and suggest the most reliable route.
