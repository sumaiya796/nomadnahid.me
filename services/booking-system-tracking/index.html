What I do

What's included.

  • Work out how your booking tool records a completed booking: thank-you page, event, embed or redirect
  • Track booking-page visits, booking starts and completed bookings
  • Handle embedded and third-party booking widgets and cross-domain journeys where possible
  • Send completed bookings to GA4 and Google Ads as conversions, with values where available
  • Track calls and forms alongside bookings, so every enquiry counts
  • Validate each event in GA4 DebugView

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Map the journey

    I walk through your booking flow as a customer would, from ad to confirmation.

  2. Find the signal

    I identify the most reliable point that proves a booking happened.

  3. Implement

    Events and conversions are built in GTM, GA4 and Google Ads.

  4. Validate

    Test bookings are checked end to end in DebugView and Google Ads.

FAQ

Common questions.

My booking tool opens in a pop-up or another domain. Can it still be tracked?

Often, yes, through the tool's own events, a redirect to a thank-you page or cross-domain setup. Some tools limit what can be tracked; I tell you what is possible before we start.

Which booking tools have you tracked?

Shopify and Halaxy for a clinic, a hotel booking engine, and WordPress and Webflow booking tools. Each one is a little different, so I test the full flow every time.

Can I see bookings by campaign in Google Ads?

Yes, once completed bookings are tracked as a conversion, you can see bookings by campaign, keyword and ad.
