FAQ

Common questions.

Can you fix a GA4 setup someone else started?

Yes. I audit what exists, keep what works and fix or remove what doesn't, so you don't lose historical data unnecessarily.

Should I import GA4 key events into Google Ads?

Sometimes. For many accounts I prefer native Google Ads conversion tags for bidding and use GA4 for analysis. I recommend what fits your setup.

Do you set up e-commerce tracking in GA4?

Yes: view item, add to cart, begin checkout and purchase with values, on Shopify, WooCommerce and other platforms.
