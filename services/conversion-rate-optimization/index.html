What I do

What's included.

  • Review landing pages against the searches and ads that send traffic to them
  • Tighten the headline, offer and call to action so they match buying intent
  • Simplify forms and make calling or booking easy on mobile
  • Check page speed and mobile layout issues that cost conversions
  • Use GA4 and Microsoft Clarity data to see where visitors drop off
  • Suggest tests and measure the change against real conversions

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Measure

    I confirm conversions are tracked, so we can see whether changes help.

  2. Review

    I go through the page, the ads and the visitor behaviour data.

  3. Recommend

    You get a prioritised list of page and ad changes.

  4. Test

    Changes go live one at a time where possible, and results are compared.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you build landing pages?

I focus on reviewing and improving pages: structure, copy, forms and calls to action. I can work with your developer or page builder to apply the changes.

How do you know if a change worked?

By comparing tracked conversions and conversion rate before and after, or with a split test when traffic allows.

Is CRO only about the website?

No. Often the gain comes from matching keywords and ads to the page more closely, as well as from the page itself.
