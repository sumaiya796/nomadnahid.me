What I do

What's included.

  • Structure campaigns by service, so each ad matches what the customer searched
  • Target your real service area and the times people actually call
  • Track phone calls, call clicks and form submissions as conversions
  • Cut irrelevant searches such as jobs, DIY and free queries with negative keywords
  • Write ads that match the service, location and offer on your landing page
  • Move budget to the services and areas with the lowest cost per lead

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Understand the business

    Services, service area, average job value and what a good lead costs for you.

  2. Track leads

    Calls and forms are set up as conversions and tested before launch.

  3. Launch by service

    Separate ad groups or campaigns per service, with tight keywords and location targeting.

  4. Refine

    Search terms, budgets and schedules are tuned toward booked work.

Who it's for

Is this a fit for you?

Local and regional service businesses, such as dental practices, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, repair shops and home services, that want a steady flow of calls and enquiries.

Most of my clients are in the US and Europe, but I work with local service businesses wherever Google Ads runs.
