What I do

What's included.

  • Set up or clean up Google Merchant Center and fix feed errors and disapprovals

  • Build Performance Max asset groups and Shopping campaigns around product lines and margins

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Check feed and tracking

    I review Merchant Center, the product feed and purchase tracking before touching budgets.

  2. Structure

    Products are grouped into campaigns and asset groups by category, price or margin.

  3. Launch

    Shopping and Performance Max go live with value-based bidding and clear audience signals.

  4. Scale

    I move budget to the products and asset groups that return the most sales value.

FAQ

Common questions.

Should my store use Performance Max or Standard Shopping?

It depends on your catalogue, budget and data. Many stores do well with Performance Max on a clean feed; Standard Shopping gives more control for testing or tight budgets. I recommend a mix after reviewing your account.

Can you fix Merchant Center disapprovals?

Yes. I review feed errors, policy issues and missing attributes, fix what can be fixed in the feed or store, and tell you plainly about anything that needs a change on the website.

Do you work with WooCommerce as well as Shopify?

Yes. I set up the feed and purchase tracking for both, so conversion value in Google Ads reflects actual orders.
