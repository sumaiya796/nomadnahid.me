What I do

What's included.

  • Plan the account structure around your services or products and the searches buyers actually use
  • Build and manage Search, Performance Max, Shopping, Demand Gen, YouTube and Display campaigns
  • Review search terms regularly and add negatives so budget goes to buying intent
  • Adjust bids, budgets and bidding strategies as conversion data builds up
  • Test ad copy and creative assets, and pause what doesn't perform
  • Report on leads, sales, cost per result and ROAS in plain English

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Review

    I look at your account, tracking, landing pages and goals, and agree the numbers that matter.

  2. Fix the basics

    Conversion tracking is checked first, so bidding learns from real leads and sales.

  3. Build

    Campaigns are structured by service or product line, with keywords, ads and negatives in place.

  4. Optimise

    I review search terms, budgets and assets regularly and move spend to what converts.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which campaign types do you manage?

Search, Performance Max, Shopping, Demand Gen, YouTube and Display. I choose the mix based on your goal, budget and how much conversion data the account already has.

Do I keep ownership of my Google Ads account?

Yes. I request access from my manager account, you approve it, and everything I build stays in your account. You can remove my access at any time.

How do you report results?

I report on the numbers that matter to the business: leads or sales, cost per result and ROAS, with notes on what changed and what I plan next.
