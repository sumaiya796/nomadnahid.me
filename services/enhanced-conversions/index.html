FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between Enhanced Conversions for web and for leads?

For web improves measurement of conversions that happen on your site, such as purchases or form fills. For leads helps measure leads that turn into sales later, offline, by matching them back to the ad click.

Is Enhanced Conversions privacy-safe?

Data is hashed before it is sent, as Google requires. You are still responsible for your consent setup and privacy policy, and I explain what to check; I'm not a lawyer, so I can't give legal advice.

Do I need conversion tracking working first?

Yes. Enhanced Conversions improves an existing conversion setup, so I check and fix the base tracking first if needed.
