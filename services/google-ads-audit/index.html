What I do

What's included.

  • Review account structure, campaign types, bidding strategies and budgets
  • Check search terms and keywords for wasted spend and missed intent
  • Verify conversion tracking: what is counted, what is missing and what is double-counted
  • Look at the journey from ad to landing page to lead or purchase
  • Compare CPA and ROAS across campaigns, devices, locations and times
  • Deliver a written action plan ordered by impact

How I handle it

Step by step.

  1. Access

    You give read access to Google Ads (and GA4 or GTM if relevant). No passwords needed.

  2. Review

    I go through the account, tracking and landing pages against your business goals.

  3. Report

    You get a written summary of what is working, what is wasting money and why.

  4. Plan

    We go through the fixes on a call, and you choose whether to apply them yourself or with me.

Who it's for

Businesses whose Google Ads feel expensive or flat, anyone taking over an account from an agency, and owners who want a second opinion before raising budget.

No. The action plan is yours to use however you like. Some clients apply it themselves; others ask me to.

No. I send an access request from my Google Ads manager account, and you can remove access at any time.
