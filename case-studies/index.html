Case 01 · E-commerce · Performance Max · Featured

$911 turned into $79.7K in sales value: 87x ROAS (conversion value) with Performance Max

8,746.56% Actual ROAS (87.47x conversion value / cost) on $911 spend

$911 ad spend → $79.7K in sales value.

US niche online store selling pacifiers, diapers, playwear and clothing (adult-size specialty products) · Performance Max (retail / product feed) · Apr 1 – Sep 30, 2026 (6 months)

Challenge

A specialty online store with four distinct product lines (pacifiers, play items, diapers and clothing) needed profitable US sales on a very small budget of $15 a day. A single catch-all campaign would blur which products actually sell and let Google spend on the wrong shoppers, so the account needed a structure that kept every dollar tied to product intent and to real purchase value, not clicks.

What I did

  1. Built one Performance Max campaign on the store's product feed so Google could serve Shopping, Search, YouTube, Display, Discover and Gmail inventory from a single, value-optimized budget.
  2. Split the campaign into four product-category asset groups (Pacifiers, Play, Diapers, Clothing), each with its own images, videos, headlines and listing groups matched to that category.
  3. Fed each asset group tightly matched search themes (e.g. pacifier, soother and binky terms for Pacifiers; diaper terms for Diapers; onesie and clothing terms for Clothing) plus first-party 'Your data' audience signals, giving the algorithm clear intent from day one.
  4. Targeted the United States only, matching ad delivery to the store's shipping market.
  5. Optimized toward purchase conversion value and tracked ROAS, not clicks, so bidding chased the highest-value orders.
  6. Reviewed performance by asset group (cost, conversion value, conversion rate, cost per conversion) to see which product lines earned the most per dollar.
  7. Kept the account at a 100% optimization score while holding spend to $15 a day.

Case 02 · Lead Generation · US dental practice

784 new patient leads in 30 days, at $28.51 per lead

784.43 conversions in 30 days at $28.51 cost per conversion

$22.4K ad spend → 784 patient leads (calls and appointment requests).

US dental practice (general, emergency, implant, denture, orthodontic & Spanish-language care) · Search (service-themed: Dentist, Emergency, Implants, Dentures, Braces, Hygiene, Spanish), Search (branded & competitor), Performance Max (Dental Implants, Braces, Emergency), call assets / call bid adjustments, ad scheduling · Sep 14 – Oct 13, 2026 (30 days), with drill-downs for Oct 12–14, 2026

Challenge

A busy dental practice was running 13 Google Ads campaigns across general dentistry, emergency care, implants, dentures, braces, hygiene and a Spanish-language audience, plus Performance Max. With roughly $720/day in budget spread across many services, the practice needed to know which services, keywords, audiences and hours were actually producing patient leads (calls and appointment requests), cut spend on irrelevant searches, and keep cost per lead under control while several campaigns were limited by budget.

What I did

  1. Audited the full account: 13 campaigns, their ad groups, bidding and budgets, using the Overview, campaign and ad-group reports to spot which services drove leads and which campaigns were budget-limited.
  2. Reviewed keyword targeting and search intent across 2,237 clicks of search traffic, prioritising high-intent terms such as 'dental implants near me', 'affordable dental implants' and 'denture cleaning', and flagging low-Quality-Score and off-intent terms for negatives.
  3. Organised the structure by service theme (Dentist, Emergency, Implants, Dentures, Braces, Hygiene, Spanish) plus a separate branded & competitor campaign, so every search, ad and landing message lines up with one service and performance is easy to read.
  4. Evaluated ads and assets: responsive search ad copy, sitelinks (Book an Appointment, New Patient Specials, Dental Bridges), callouts, call assets and logos, measuring clicks, conversions and cost/conv per asset.
  5. Analysed phone-call performance and call bid adjustments: calls converted at 73.57% for $14.65 per conversion, well below the account's $25.66 CPA for the same window, making calls the most efficient lead source to lean into.
  6. Broke down demographics (age, gender, household income) and the ad schedule by day and hour to find the most efficient segments, e.g. ages 35–44 in the Dentist campaign at $7.29 per conversion and Wednesday daytime Dentist ads at $7.49.
  7. Validated conversion tracking (calls, appointment requests, lead forms) so optimisation decisions are based on real patient enquiries rather than clicks.
